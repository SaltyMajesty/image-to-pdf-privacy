Privacy Policy

Summary: Image to PDF & Compressor processes all images locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your images or personal data to any server.

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Image to PDF & Compressor ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Do NOT Collect

Our app is designed with your privacy in mind:

  • Images: All image processing (compression, resizing, PDF conversion) happens entirely on your device. Your images are never uploaded to any server.
  • Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, or any personal identifiers.
  • Location Data: We do not access or collect your location.
  • Contacts: We do not access your contacts.

Permissions We Request

The app requires the following permissions to function:

  • Storage/Photos Access: To select images from your gallery for compression or PDF conversion. Images are processed locally and are not transmitted anywhere.

Advertising

Our app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use data for advertising purposes according to their privacy policy.

Information that may be collected by Google AdMob includes:

  • Device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Usage data

For more information about Google's data practices, please visit: Google Privacy Policy

Data Storage

Any files created by the app (compressed images, PDFs) are stored locally on your device in your chosen location. We have no access to these files.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. The app is a utility tool that processes images locally without collecting user data.
